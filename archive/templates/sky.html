The page shows the all-sky mosaic constructed from the images from FRAM CTA-N, CTA-S0 and CTA-S1 wide-field cameras. The mosaic is a stack of abour 450,000 images, 15x15 degrees each, acquired over three years (2018-2021) from three sites (La Palma and two places at Paranal). It is stored in Hierarchical Progressive Survey (HiPS) format, and is generated with Hipsgen.jar.
